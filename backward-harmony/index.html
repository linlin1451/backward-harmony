Backward harmony

The last chord is fixed first, then the first chord of every earlier bar is chosen backward from weighted rules and from the cadences that can lead into the chord after it. Divide any bar into two to four parts; new parts hold the bar's chord until you pick something else. Swap any chord, change its inversion, then play it or take it into your DAW as MIDI.
